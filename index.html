📌 Key Lesson Takeaways for S4 ICT:
• 1NF: Ensure atomic values & eliminate repeating attribute groups.
• Functional Dependency (FD): X → Y means determinant X uniquely determines attribute Y.
• Full Functional Dependency (FFD): Attribute Y depends on the entire composite key (A, B), not just part A or part B.
• 2NF: Decompose tables to eliminate Partial Functional Dependencies on composite keys.
